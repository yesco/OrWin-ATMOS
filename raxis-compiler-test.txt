=============================== MeteoriC-65 ================================

(2026-11-26: ./ptest '??-*' in OrwinClaude, ptest-rax.out. Keep updated.)

How they're run (ptest, each as test-rax-NN-name):

- their #include "..." are inlined
- <conio.h> <types.h> are "conio.h" "types.h" (libmos6502vm's)
- cc65 and oscar: also their _puts.c, and a _putc that is putchar
- MeteoriC-65 has its own: _putc _puts peek poke deek doke MEMPTR,
  uint8_t int8_t uint16_t int16_t
- the expected output: their own gcc command (tools/test-samples.sh)

The backends (cycles, sim65 / oscar's emulator):

- ivm asm fix: MeteoriC-65's bytecode on the C VM / asm VM / fixed asm VM
- zpjit: the JIT's 6502 image (mem[] in zero page), hjit: the same JIT on
  the host's 6502 sim
- cc65 -Os, oscar64 -Os: for comparison (with their I/O, above)

  -: not compiled, FAIL: no result, *: the output differs

may follow behind them).

  - short long (sizeof(short), sizeof(long))
  - LF CR: conio.h's #define (gone with the skipped include)
  - '\x0a': char escapes
  - _div16u _modr16u: compiler helpers, not C (cc65 and oscar fail too)

  - runs

  - runs

03-bytecpy
  - *(char *)E = v: a store through a cast

04-memcopy
  - a global initializer with an expression: MEMPTR(0x4000)
  - for (...; ++i)

05-0xcafe
  - LF: conio.h's #define

06-sieve
  - an array size expression: primes[number + 1] (then primes is
    undefined everywhere)

07-aes256
  - ^= (and the other op=)
  - for (...; i += 4)
  - prototypes (static void xprints(const char *s);)
  - typedef struct, & of a struct variable

08-mandelbrot

  - negative literals (-100)
  - for (...; i += 6)
